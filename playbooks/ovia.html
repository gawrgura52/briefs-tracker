Ovia Winners Playbook

The Ovia version of the Garlic and Cinnamon playbooks: every winner we have numbers for, what they share, which open cells to port first, and what's already dead. Built from Mei's Ad Database and Iteration Matrix, Kay's soap opera breakdown, the Winner Iterations board and the team vault. Like everything in the vault, these are what has worked so far and who found it, not rules.

What every Ovia winner shares

36 winners tagged on format, engine, mechanism and messenger, weighed by spend. Variables and stars are Mei's from the Iteration Matrix (★★★ proven, ★★ semi-proven).

Who we sell to

A heavier woman 50+, and someone close to her sees it

  • 67% of buyers are 60+ (survey). Nana / grandparent is a ★★★ avatar: 88, 103, 114, 126, 158.
  • The hero is chubby, less conventionally attractive, never tired-looking (131, 418 Dawn).
  • Story ads star the woman hurt by someone close: husband, sister, best friend, the whole circle.
  • Men only work as half of a couple (209 dad, 100 husband) or as the B3 static.
The engines

Two families carry the account

  • Two-person skits (spike, "are you on the shot?", doctor doubts, daughter explains): .
  • Betrayal stories (husband left, sister, best friend, whole circle knew): , as natives, songs and soaps.
  • MOF: 193 Exposing Fakes is the single biggest spender ($163k).
The format

The story wins again in a new format

  • 136 native → 270 soap. 201 native → 263 song. 235 native → 412 song. 292 native → 370 video. 351 animation → 352 soap.
  • Hold ceiling (Mei): soap 39, song 38, skit 26, talking head 15, static 8.
  • Two people on camera held 26 to 46%. Solo was under 20%.
  • Iterations won about 14% of the time, ideation about 4% (Mei's count).
The hook

The other person opens, or two numbers and a relationship

  • Skits: an action or question from the second person. 254 "Mom, turn to the side" 74%, 88 "I'm gonna go to the bathroom" 70%, 160 "I found this in your nightstand" 71%.
  • Stories: two numbers + a relationship in line one. "My husband of 31 years left me for a 29 year old."
  • Doctor doubts the result (126 63%, 418 68%). 418 opens on the number (186).
  • Readable at first glance, humiliation in front of a group (Mei).
The mechanism

One breath, before the first result

  • Full signal for stories: "Your body quit sending you the full signal a few years ago." .
  • Peer-vague for skits: "It's got that GLP-1 activation... I don't fully get it." .
  • Serotonin only has the best CPAs: 418 $28, 244 $27.5 ("that's why you're in the pantry at nine").
  • 40 to 70 words, said once. Called a cutting mix / pink drink, never a supplement.
The close

Proof first, then the product, then remove the risk

  • Proof happens on camera before any claim (she can't finish the plate, clothes fall off).
  • Product shows after the proof. Every ad that named it in the first 5 seconds held under 13%.
  • Kay: winners say why it works before showing it, remove the risk ("What've I got to lose?" / "Worst case, a nice drink"), and end on a line to the viewer.
  • Starbucks Strawberry Acai Refresher at the reveal, scarcity close, "link below".
  • Hook and hold predict spend, not CPA. CPA sits around $36 to $43 across winners.
